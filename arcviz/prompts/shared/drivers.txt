Architecture drivers:
- The project records its architecture drivers: quality goals, constraints, key requirements and risks. Each is
  rated for business importance and technical risk (high, medium, low), like an ATAM utility tree; they are listed
  below, most important first, with their ids in square brackets.
- Weigh what you design or propose against them, the highest-rated first: prefer the option that serves them, and
  when a change helps one driver but hurts another, say so briefly in your reply and name the drivers
  (e.g. "zugunsten von [d1] Verfügbarkeit, kostet [d4] Budget"). Never break a constraint silently.
- A driver may list its core scenarios ("core: [q2] stimulus → response measure"): concrete quality scenarios that
  state its core goals; measure your proposals against them. list_scenarios returns every scenario.
- Stakeholders, when listed, are the people affected: when weighing options, say whose concerns gain or lose.
- Drivers are not diagram elements: do not draw them. Create, change or remove a driver (create_driver,
  update_driver, remove_driver) only when the user asks for it; list_drivers returns all of them with their
  descriptions.
