# First steps

New to Arcviz? Three minutes are enough to get a first diagram.

## Start from the empty project

The first time you sign in, your *Standardprojekt* is empty. The page offers one card per diagram type:

| Card | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Verteilungsdiagramm** | Where does what run: servers, networks, services and their connections |
| **Aktivitätsdiagramm** | How does a process work: steps, decisions, parallel flows |
| **Sequenzdiagramm** | Who sends what to whom, and in which order |

- **Mit Chat starten** creates an empty diagram and puts a suitable first message into the chat input. Send it as it
  is, or change it first.
- **Beispiel ansehen** creates a finished example that you can change by chat.

Everything is a normal diagram of your project; delete it when you are done.

## Talk to the chat

Describe what should exist or change, in your own words: "Add a load balancer in front of the web servers". The
assistant changes the model and the canvas follows at once. Tick **Vorschau** to see a change as a proposal first and
confirm it. Every change is saved as a version, so **Undo** and **Versionen** always take you back
([Versions](versions.md)).

## Find anything with Strg+K

Press **Strg+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) or click the search field in the top bar. Type a few letters of what you want: an
action (*Version sichern*, *Als SVG exportieren*), an area (*Architektur: Treiber*, *Anforderungen*) or a diagram of the open
project. **↑**/**↓** choose, **Enter** runs it, **Esc** closes. Only what you can do right now is offered, and the last commands
you used come first. The **⋯** menu next to **Hilfe** still holds the diagram and version actions (new, import, delete, save a
version, compare).

## Take the tour

**Hilfe → Rundgang starten** highlights the main areas of the screen one after another (navigator, chat, canvas,
review, versions). It also appears as a link on the empty project. **Esc** or **Beenden** closes it; start it again
whenever you like.
